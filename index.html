Fly over the globe

A Cessna 172 over a globe of the world. Up at cruise the world goes by a few hundred times faster than real; down low it slows so you can look around. The i bubbles only show below 3,000 feet. Fly over one to open it.

A full tank is 53 gallons, about 600 nautical miles with the 30-minute VFR reserve, so plan your legs and land at an airport to fill up. The wind is steady, and it pushes you off course: the leg card works out the heading to fly, the way an E6B flight computer does.
